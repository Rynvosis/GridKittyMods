Rebased the three situation overwrites onto 4.4 vanilla and bumped supported_version to 4.4. The overwrite files were verbatim copies of the 4.3 definitions carrying one inserted pacing modifier each, and because a whole-block overwrite wins entirely, they were quietly reverting every change Paradox made to those eight situations across two patches. Four situations regained situation_log_category, so they group under Urgent Matters and Empire Concerns in the situation log again instead of falling out of it. Genetic ascension regained its is_wilderness_empire guards, which had been the worst of it: without them a Wilderness empire hit the "genomic facility needed" hard stop with no way to build one, stalling genetic ascension permanently. Breach the Shroud now uses has_active_psi_corps instead of scanning owned planets for building_psi_corps, so alternate psi corps sources count toward stage two, reads the renamed sr_zro_found flag so the "Zro already discovered" tooltip fires, and regained the allow blocks that stop you selecting a Zro or astral thread approach you cannot pay for. Colony checks throughout moved back to vanilla's any_owned_colony from the older any_owned_planet. The mod's own logic needed nothing: all twelve ascension tradition trees behind the Patience check still exist in 4.4 with no new tree added, both patience modifiers are still valid keys, and every BASE_RATE argument still matches its vanilla base, so the pacing curve is unchanged.
